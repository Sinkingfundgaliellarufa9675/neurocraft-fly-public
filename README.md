## 🎯 What Is NeuroCraft Fly?

NeuroCraft Fly is an **interactive computer model** that lets you explore the complete wiring diagram of a fruit fly's brain inside the popular video game Minecraft. Think of it as a digital museum where you can walk through, fly around, and examine over 100,000 brain cells and their connections. Created by Evan Sinclair Smith, this project brings real neuroscience research to your computer in a fun, visual way.

You don't need to be a scientist or a programmer. If you can use a computer and enjoy exploring, you're ready to go.

## 📚 Understanding What You See

The fruit fly brain has about 100,000 neurons – tiny brain cells that send electrical signals. In NeuroCraft Fly, each neuron appears as a glowing sphere with lines branching out (these are called axons and dendrites). When two neurons connect, they form a "synapse" – shown as a bright point where lines meet. The thicker the line, the stronger the connection.

You're not just looking at random lights – this is real data from actual fly brains studied by scientists. Researchers use this exact model to understand how behavior emerges from neural circuits.
